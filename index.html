Zihan Dong

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Statistics at Rutgers University. Prior to Rutgers, I obtained my B.S. in Mathematics from the School of the Gifted Young, University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), 2023.

I am currently an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon AGI. Previously, I was a Research Intern with Qwen, Tongyi Lab, Alibaba Group. My research focuses on LLM post-training, long-horizon coding agents, and the statistical foundations of AI.

Open to research discussions, feel free to reach out!

Research

My research focuses on mid- and post-training for coding agents and the statistical foundations of large language models, including reinforcement learning, reward modeling, and evaluation from noisy feedback.

  • Just Reuse the Rollout Log-Probs: Stable Mixture-of-Experts Reinforcement Learning with a Vocabulary-KL Clip
    Zihan Dong, Shuo Yang, Chiyu Ma, Haozhe Li, Yang Zhou, Zihang Meng, Hao Zhou, Linjun Zhang, and Guoyin Wang.

    Submitted to ICLR 2027.

    VoKL stabilizes mixture-of-experts reinforcement learning by reusing rollout log-probabilities and applying a vocabulary-KL trust region to address the training–inference mismatch.

  • Evaluating LLMs When They Do Not Know the Answer: Statistical Evaluation of Mathematical Reasoning via Comparative Signals
    Zihan Dong, Zhixian Zhang, Yang Zhou, Can Jin, Ruijia Wu, Linjun Zhang.

    ICML 2026.

    Combines labeled outcomes with pairwise reasoning comparisons in a semiparametric estimator to improve the precision and reliability of LLM evaluation.

    Paper / Code

  • Alternating Reinforcement Learning for Rubric-Based Reward Modeling in Non-Verifiable LLM Post-Training
    Ran Xu, Tianci Liu, Zihan Dong, Tony Yu, Ilgee Hong, Carl Yang, Linjun Zhang, Tuo Zhao, Haoyu Wang.

    ICML 2026.

    Rubric-ARM alternates reinforcement learning updates between a rubric generator and a judge to improve reward modeling for open-ended tasks.

    Paper / Code

  • AutoResearchClaw: Self-Reinforcing Autonomous Research with Human-AI Collaboration
    Jiaqi Liu, Shi Qiu, Mairui Li, …, Zihan Dong, et al.

    Submitted to ICLR 2027.

  • Labels or Preferences? Budget-Constrained Learning with Human Judgments over AI-Generated Outputs
    Zihan Dong, Xiaotian Hou, Ruijia Wu, and Linjun Zhang.

    Preprint, 2026.
